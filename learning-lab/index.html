    StackCalc Learning Lab

    The Learning Lab bridges Jerome Bruner’s Concrete-Representational-Abstract (CRA) framework with the mechanical clarity of Reverse Polish Notation (RPN): 1. Concrete: Physical 3D-printed Gridfinity math manipulatives on classroom desks. 2. Representational (2D Interactive Lab Below): Virtual proportional blocks and place-value trays for interactive whiteboards and 1:1 Chromebooks. 3. Abstract: Dedicated 4-line operational stack registers on the SC-6 Elementary and SC-32 Scientific handheld calculators.

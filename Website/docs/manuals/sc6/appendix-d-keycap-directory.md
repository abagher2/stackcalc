### How the Three Labels on Every Button & Number-Pad Shortcuts Work
Look closely at any button on your SC-6 and you will spot up to three labels—plus a clever spatial pattern hidden across the number pad (`1`–`9`). The bold **Primary Legend** printed on the keycap face runs with a single tap. Tapping **`{{key:f}}`** once lights the `↰` indicator in the top-left corner of the screen and selects the **upper-left `{{key:f}}` label** on your next key press; tapping **`{{key:g}}`** once lights `↱` and selects the **upper-right `{{key:g}}` label** (and tapping the same shift key a second time cancels it if you change your mind).

Even the number pad (`1`–`9` and `-`) is organized as a visual map so your fingers can find geometry builders and measurement conversions without hunting:

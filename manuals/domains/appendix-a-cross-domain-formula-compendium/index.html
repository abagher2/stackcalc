    Appendix A: Cross-Domain Formula & Equation Compendium

    This compendium consolidates the governing mathematical identities, physical constants, four-register RPN stack conventions, and bidirectional Equation Solver Workspace schemas across all nine specialized instruments in the StackCalc Domain Series (SC-35, SC-14, SC-16, SC-24, SC-50, SC-60, SC-78, SC-80, and SC-95). Each section summarizes the first-principles equations derived across Units I through IX (Chapters 1 through 27) so engineers, navigators, clinicians, and analysts can verify input dimensions, immediate stack arguments, and closed-form or numerical solver relationships at a glance.

    Universal Three-Pillar Stack, Precision, and Bidirectional Solver Conventions

    Every instrument in the StackCalc Domain Series shares a common three-pillar computational architecture backed by IEEE 754 double-precision floating-point arithmetic (\(64\text{-bit}\) word, \(53\text{-bit}\) binary significand, providing \(15\) to \(17\) significant decimal digits) and selectable fixed-width integer arithmetic on the SC-16:

    1. Configurable RPN Stack Depth ([4-LV], [8-LV], or [INF] via [g] [FLAGS]): Practitioners can operate with a classic four-register \((X, Y, Z, T)\) operational stack ([4-LV]), expand to an eight-register stack ([8-LV]), or enable unbounded stack capacity ([INF]) for multi-branch field derivations. Across all stack configurations, the active four-register window at the bottom of the stack (Register T, Register Z, Register Y, Register X) obeys deterministic RPN lift and drop semantics:
    Register Operational Role Behavior on Binary Operation ([+], [-], [×], [÷], [yˣ]) Behavior on [ENTER]
    Register \(T\) Top / 4th Visible Level Replicates downward into \(Z\) (\(T' = T\)) in [4-LV] mode (or drops from R5 in [8-LV]/[INF]) Receives prior \(Z\) (\(T' = Z\))
    Register \(Z\) 3rd Stack Level Drops into \(Y\) (\(Z' = T\)) Receives prior \(Y\) (\(Z' = Y\))
    Register \(Y\) 2nd Operand Level Consumed as left operand (\(Y \circ X\)); receives prior \(Z\) Replicates \(X\) (\(Y' = X\))
    Register \(X\) Active Display Line Consumed as right operand; receives result \(f(Y, X)\) Remains in \(X\) (lift disabled for next digit)

    2.     3. Bidirectional Equation Solver Workspace ([g] [SOLVE] / [XEQ] + Softkeys [LFU0]–[LFU5]): Opening a domain key's Equation Workspace renders the governing equation across Line 1 of the LCD and binds the six top-row softkeys ([LFU0]–[LFU5]) to its symbolic variables. Storing known values in any order and pressing [g] [SOLVE] (or [XEQ]) followed by the target variable softkey solves the governing law forward or backward for any unknown parameter via exact algebraic isolation or Secant–Brent numerical root-finding.
    4. Domain Modes, Native Input Parsers, and Live Reference Ledgers ([g] [▦]): Native domain entry modes ([g] [▸HMS] via [f] [▸HR]/[g] [▸HMS], [f] [f-i] via [f] [f-i], [HEX]/[f] [DEC]/[f] [OCT]/[g] [BIN] + [g] [WSZ] + [f] [NEG], [TC] + [FPS], chemical formula entry via [Z]) and the non-destructive Reference Table ([g] [▦]) maintain live domain ledgers (@COGO, @BK%, @PAT/@MAR, and bivariate statistical accumulators).

    Geomatics, Land Surveying, and Flight Navigation

    Covered in Unit I, Chapters 1–3, the SC-35 geomatics and flight-navigation instrument links sexagesimal angular encoding (DDD.MMSS via [f] [▸HR] and [g] [▸HMS]), plane coordinate geometry ([TRV], [INV], [f] [PT+], [g] [CLS]), horizontal and vertical highway curves ([CRV], [f] [VCV]), differential leveling ([f] [LVL]), and atmospheric flight mechanics ([TSD], [WND], [g] [XWC], [f] [ALT], [f] [CG]).

    Sexagesimal Angle Conversion and Horizontal/Vertical Curves

    Packed sexagesimal format (DDD.MMSS) encodes integer degrees \(D\), arc-minutes \(M \in [0, 59]\), and arc-seconds \(S \in [0, 59.99]\) as \(D + M/100 + S/10{,}000\), converting to decimal degrees \(\theta_{\text{dec}}\) ([f] [▸HR], with inverse [g] [▸HMS]), while the [CRV] ([CRV]) Equation Workspace (L = R × D × π / 180 on [LFU0]–[LFU2]: [L], [R], [D]) relates circular arc length \(L\), radius \(R\), tangent \(T = R\tan(D/2)\), and central deflection angle \(D\):

    \[\theta_{\text{dec}} = D + \frac{M}{60} + \frac{S}{3600}, \qquad L = R \cdot D_{\text{deg}} \cdot \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}, \qquad T = R \tan\!\left(\frac{D}{2}\right)\]

    Forward and Inverse Coordinate Geometry

    Given a known survey station \(A\) at coordinates \((N_1, E_1)\), horizontal leg distance \(D\), and clockwise North azimuth \(\alpha\), forward traverse ([TRV]) computes the target station \((N_2, E_2)\), whereas inverse traverse ([INV]) recovers \(D\) and \(\alpha\) from coordinate differences \(\Delta N = N_2 - N_1\) and \(\Delta E = E_2 - E_1\):

    \[N_2 = N_1 + D \cos\alpha, \qquad E_2 = E_1 + D \sin\alpha, \qquad D = \sqrt{(\Delta N)^2 + (\Delta E)^2}, \qquad \alpha = \operatorname{atan2}(\Delta E, \Delta N) \pmod{360^\circ}\]

    Bowditch Compass Rule Traverse Adjustment

    For a closed polygon traverse ([f] [PT+], [g] [CLS], and the @COGO ledger in [g] [▦]) of total perimeter \(P = \sum D_i\) with linear misclosure \(E_{\text{mis}} = \sqrt{(\Sigma \Delta N)^2 + (\Sigma \Delta E)^2}\), the Bowditch compass rule distributes closure corrections \((\delta N_i, \delta E_i)\) in proportion to leg length \(D_i\):

    \[\delta N_i = -\Sigma \Delta N \cdot \frac{D_i}{P}, \qquad \delta E_i = -\Sigma \Delta E \cdot \frac{D_i}{P}, \qquad \text{Precision Ratio} = 1 : \frac{P}{E_{\text{mis}}}\]

    Aviation Wind Triangle, Crosswind, and Density Altitude

    With runway or course heading \(\psi\), wind direction \(\theta_w\) (\(A = \theta_w - \psi\)), wind speed \(V_w\), and true airspeed \(V_{\text{TAS}}\), the wind-triangle solver ([WND], opening the [WND] workspace X = V × SIN(A) on [X], [V], [A]) and [g] [XWC] decompose crosswind \(V_{\text{XW}}\), headwind \(V_{\text{HW}}\), and wind correction angle \(\text{WCA}\), while the atmospheric solver ([f] [ALT], opening the [f] [ALT] workspace D = P + 120 × (T - 15) on [D], [P], [T]) computes Pressure Altitude \(P\) (in feet) and Density Altitude \(D\) from field elevation \(\text{Elev}\), altimeter setting \(\text{Alt}_{\text{inHg}}\), outside air temperature \(T\) (\(^\circ\text{C}\)), and standard temperature \(T_{\text{ISA}} = 15^\circ\text{C} - 2^\circ\text{C} \times (P / 1000\text{ ft})\):

    \[V_{\text{XW}} = V_w \sin(\theta_w - \psi), \qquad V_{\text{HW}} = V_w \cos(\theta_w - \psi), \qquad \text{WCA} = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{V_{\text{XW}}}{V_{\text{TAS}}}\right)\]
    \[P = \text{Elev} + 1000 \times (29.92 - \text{Alt}_{\text{inHg}}), \qquad D \approx P + 120 \times (T - T_{\text{ISA}})\]

    Three-Phase AC Power, Conductor Voltage Drop, and Control Valve Flow

    For a balanced load with line-to-line voltage \(V_{LL}\), line current \(I_L\), power factor \(F = \cos\phi\), one-way conduit run length \(L\) (in feet), conductor cross-section \(\text{CM}\) (in circular mils), and resistivity constant \(K\) (\(12.9\,\Omega\cdot\text{CM/ft}\) for copper or \(21.2\,\Omega\cdot\text{CM/ft}\) for aluminum), active power ([PF] Equation Workspace P = V × I × F, alongside [g] [FLA] and [g] [kVA]) and conductor voltage drop ([AWG] Equation Workspace D = 2 × R × L × I / 1000 on [LFU0]–[LFU4]: [D], [R], [L], [I], [G], plus control valve flow Q = C × √(P / G) under [g] [Cᵥ]) are:

    \[P_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} \cdot V_{LL} \cdot I_L \cdot \cos\phi, \qquad V_{\text{drop}, 3\phi} = \frac{\sqrt{3} \cdot K \cdot I_L \cdot L}{\text{CM}}, \qquad D = \frac{2 \cdot K \cdot I_L \cdot L}{\text{CM}} = \frac{2 \cdot R_{\Omega/\text{kft}} \cdot L \cdot I_L}{1000}\]

    Covered in Unit III, Chapters 7–9, the SC-16 systems and cybersecurity instrument operates on selectable fixed-width integer words (8-bit, 16-bit, 32-bit, 64-bit via [g] [WSZ] and unsigned mode [g] [UNS]), radix bases ([HEX], [f] [DEC], [f] [OCT], [g] [BIN]), IEEE 754 binary floating-point encodings ([FLT], [f] [F16], [g] [F64]), embedded timer/ADC peripherals ([PWM], [g] [BPS], [f] [ADC], [g] [DAC], [f] [τ]), and network addressing ([NET], [f] [CRC], [g] [MOD]).

    Two's Complement Signed Integer Representation and Bitwise Operators

    In an \(N\)-bit word (\(N \in \{8, 16, 32, 64\}\), cycled via [g] [WSZ]), a binary bit vector \(b_{N-1}b_{N-2}\dots b_1 b_0\) represents the signed integer value \(V_{\text{signed}}\), where two's complement negation ([f] [NEG] / [+/-]) adds \(1\) to the bitwise one's complement ([g] [~]) modulo \(2^N\), and shift/mask operators ([≪], [≫], [f] [ASR], [g] [ROR], [f] [SB], [f] [CB], [g] [B?], [f] [MSK], [&], [|], [⊕], [f] [~&], [f] [~|], [f] [#B], [g] [CLZ]) manipulate individual register fields:

    \[V_{\text{signed}} = -b_{N-1} 2^{N-1} + \sum_{k=0}^{N-2} b_k 2^k, \qquad -x \equiv (\sim x + 1) \pmod{2^N}\]

    IEEE 754 Single-Precision Floating-Point Layout and Embedded Timer/PWM Synthesis

    For a 32-bit float32 word ([FLT], paired with byte-order swap [g] [SWP]) partitioned into sign bit \(s \in \{0, 1\}\) (bit 31), 8-bit biased exponent \(E \in [1, 254]\) (bits 30–23), and 23-bit significand mantissa \(M\) (bits 22–0), the real value \(x\) and the hardware timer PWM frequency ([PWM] Equation Workspace F = C / (P × A) binding [F], [C], [P], [A] on [LFU0]–[LFU3], alongside [g] [BPS] divisor D = C / (16 × B)) are:

    \[x = (-1)^s \times \left(1 + \frac{M}{2^{23}}\right) \times 2^{E - 127}, \qquad F = \frac{C}{P \cdot A}, \qquad \text{BRR} = \frac{f_{\text{clk}}}{16 \times f_{\text{baud}}}\]

    Mixed-Signal ADC Quantization, RC Settling, Modular Cryptography, and CIDR

    An \(N\)-bit successive-approximation ADC ([f] [ADC] Equation Workspace D = V / R × 2^N binding [D], [V], [R], [N] on [LFU0]–[LFU3]) maps analog input \(V\) against reference \(R\) with ideal signal-to-noise ratio \(\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = 6.0206\,N + 1.7609\text{ dB}\) and Effective Number of Bits \(\text{ENOB} = (\text{SINAD} - 1.7609)/6.0206\), while the RC anti-aliasing filter ([f] [τ] [f] [τ] Equation Workspace T = R × C binding [T], [R], [C] on [LFU0]–[LFU2]) requires \(t_{\text{acq}} \ge (N + 1)\ln(2)\,\tau\) to settle within \(\frac{1}{2}\text{ LSB}\). In network and cryptographic analysis ([g] [MOD], [f] [CRC], [NET], [f] [▸KiB], [f] [▸MHz], [g] [▸ns]), modular arithmetic, Shannon entropy \(H(X)\), and IPv4 subnet host capacity follow:

    \[D = \left\lfloor \frac{V}{R} \cdot 2^N \right\rfloor, \qquad t_{\text{acq}} \ge (N + 1)\ln(2)\,R C, \qquad C = M^e \pmod n, \qquad H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^{m} p_i \log_2 p_i, \qquad N_{\text{hosts}} = 2^{32 - P} - 2\]

    Cinema Optics, Broadcast Timecode, and Sound

    Covered in Unit IV, Chapters 10–12, the SC-24 motion-picture and broadcast calculator unites SMPTE frame-accurate timecode arithmetic ([TC], [FPS], [g] [DF], [g] [+TC], [g] [-TC]), geometrical camera optics ([FOV], [DOF], [f] [HFD]), rotary shutter exposure ([EV], [f] [SHT], [LUX], [g] [GEL]), and acoustic and data propagation ([dB], [f] [SPL], [g] [DLY], [BIT]).

    SMPTE Timecode and NTSC Drop-Frame Compensation

    Color NTSC broadcast video runs at \(f_{\text{NTSC}} = 30 \times 1000 / 1001 \approx 29.97002997\text{ fps}\), causing a nominal \(30\text{ fps}\) frame counter to drift by \(+108\text{ frames}\) (\(+3.6\text{ s}\)) per hour. SMPTE 29.97 DF Drop-Frame timecode ([TC], [FPS], [g] [DF], with [TC] Equation Workspace F = S × R on [F], [S], [R]) reconciles wall-clock time by skipping frame labels :00 and :01 at the start of every minute except minutes divisible by \(10\):

    \[N_{\text{dropped/hr}} = 60 \times 2 - 6 \times 2 = 108\text{ frames/hr}, \qquad F = S \times R\]

    Hyperfocal Distance, Depth of Field, and Rotary Shutter Exposure

    For lens focal length \(F\), sensor width \(W\), aperture \(N\) (\(f\)-stop or photometric \(T\)-stop), sensor circle of confusion \(C\), focus distance \(d\), frame rate \(R\), and rotary shutter angle \(\theta_{\text{shutter}}\) (in degrees), the optical solvers ([FOV] A = 2 × ATAN(W / (2 × F)), [DOF] H = F² / (N × C), [EV] N² = T × 2^E, [f] [SHT], and [LUX] E = I / D²) evaluate field of view \(A\), hyperfocal distance \(H\), near and far depth-of-field limits \(D_{\text{near}}\) and \(D_{\text{far}}\), and frame exposure time \(T\):

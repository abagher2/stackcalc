    Appendix B: Domain Instrument Keycap & Workspace Directory

    This directory catalogs the physical keycaps, Row 0 domain softkeys, yellow ([↰]) and blue ([↱]) shift layers, bidirectional Equation Solver Workspaces ([g] [SOLVE] / [XEQ]), and register input-output signatures across all nine instruments in the StackCalc Domain Series (SC-35 through SC-95). Each table lists the pre-execution stack arguments consumed from Register T, Register Z, Register Y, and Register X alongside the post-execution results returned to Register X' and Register Y' and the softkey variable bindings inside each instrument's Equation Solver Workspace.

    Every calculator in the StackCalc Domain Series provides configurable RPN stack depth ([4-LV], [8-LV], or [INF] cycled on Flag 5 5:DEP inside [↱] [FLAGS]), dual-mode domain keys that combine one-key RPN stack evaluation with a bidirectional Equation Solver Workspace ([g] [SOLVE] / [XEQ]), and non-destructive Live Reference Ledgers ([g] [▦]) paired with ten general-purpose memory registers (R₀–R₉):

    Dynamic Live Reference Ledgers ([g] [▦])

    Pressing [g] [▦] ([↱] [+/-]) opens the non-destructive domain reference ledger. Unlike a static table of physical constants, the StackCalc ledger dynamically tracks data accumulated in real time with [f] [PT+] or [Σ+]:

    • SC-35 (@COGO): Accumulates polygon perimeter coordinates, calculates cumulative linear misclosure (CLOS), and computes Gauss's Shoelace polygon area (AREA) in square feet, acres, and hectares.
    • SC-14 (@BOM / Service Sizing): Sums individual branch circuit loads via [Σ+], calculates total load (TOT LOAD), applies the NEC 125% continuous duty multiplier (80% CONT), and automatically selects the minimum standard service panel rating (PNL MIN: 100A, 125A, 200A, 400A).
    • SC-16 (Registers & Cyber): Decomposes the active register into simultaneous HEX, DEC, OCT, and BIN representations, extracts IEEE-754 32/64-bit sign, exponent, and mantissa bitfields, formats fixed-point Q16.16, and calculates 4-byte and 8-byte struct alignment padding (ALIGN 4B, ALIGN 8B).
    • SC-24 (Cinema Production Log): Logs scene takes with [Σ+], tracking total accumulated timecode (TOTAL TC), total frames (TOTAL F), take count (TAKES), average take duration (AVG TAKE), camera raw data generated (DATA GEN in GB), and remaining card recording runtime (512G REM in hours).
    • SC-50 (Smith & Friis Cascade): Evaluates complex reflection parameters (|GAMMA|, GAMMA ANG, VSWR, RET LOSS, normalized \(r/z_0\) and \(x/z_0\)), and sums multi-stage RF amplifiers via [Σ+] to compute total cascaded gain (CASC G) and Friis cascaded noise figure (CASC NF).
    • SC-60 (Solution & Formula Mass Breakdown): Accumulates gravimetric solute masses via [Σ+] to display solute mass percent (SOL1 %, TOT MASS), and parses active chemical formulas (FML, MW) into live per-element mass percentage breakdowns (H MASS%, O MASS%, etc.).
    • SC-78 (Baking Formula Ledger): Anchors formula flour weight (FLOUR), computes live Baker's percentages for all added ingredients (ING1 %, ING2 %), and tracks total batch dough weight (TOT W).
    • SC-80 (Patient Clinical Dashboard): Displays the persistent Patient Profile: patient weight (PAT WT), height (PAT HT), age and sex (PAT AGE), serum creatinine (PAT SCR), Mosteller Body Surface Area (PAT BSA), Devine Ideal Body Weight (PAT IBW), and Cockcroft–Gault creatinine clearance (PAT GFR).
    • SC-95 (Bivariate Statistics & Regression): Accumulates \((x, y)\) sample points via [Σ+], tracking sample count (\(n\)), means (\(\bar{x}, \bar{y}\)), standard deviations (\(s_x, s_y\)), ordinary least-squares slope (\(m\)), intercept (\(b\)), Pearson correlation (\(r\)), coefficient of determination (\(r^2\)), and the last 10 points.

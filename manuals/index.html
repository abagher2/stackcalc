    Why We Process District Orders via Formal PO Requisition:

    1. Public Procurement Law Compliance: US public school districts and state universities operate under state procurement codes requiring vendor approval, formal Net-30 purchase orders, and payment via district check or direct ACH, rather than consumer credit cards.
    2. Vendor Packet & Paperwork: We furnish complete vendor registration packets upon request:
    3. Form W-9 (Signed Taxpayer Identification Number)
    4. EFT / ACH Direct Deposit authorization form
    5. Certificate of Insurance (COI)
    6. State vendor portal registration (Munis, Skyward, Texas BuyBoard, SAM.gov UEI)
    7. Sole-Source Justification: StackCalc RPN calculators and patented tactile manipulatives are proprietary educational apparatus. We provide signed Sole Source Justification Letters enabling districts to bypass mandatory multi-vendor competitive bidding.
    8. Zero Hidden Transaction Surcharges: Direct PO purchasing saves school districts credit card processing fees and allows educational tax-exempt certification.

## 🔍 How to Identify Your Missing Piece

Every StackCalc physical manipulative kit and calculator Maker Kit includes a heavy-weight **4×6″ Archival Reference Card** seated beneath the storage box lid:

1. **Check the Reference Card:** Look at the exploded CAD schematic on the card to identify the missing component.
2. **Find the Part ID:** Each component has a designated Part ID code (for example: `PLACE-TILE-7`, `FRAC-TOW-4`, `ALGB-UNIT-POS`, `BASE10-COUNTER`, `SC32-BATT-DOOR`).
3. **Choose Your Replacement Route:**
   - **Have a 3D printer in your school or makerspace?** Print it for free in minutes from [Printables](https://www.printables.com/@StackCalc_5593834).
   - **No 3D printer available?** Order factory-printed refills on-demand through our [Slant 3D Portal](https://teleportpod.com/portals/profile/StackCalc).

## 💡 Quick Tips for Teachers & Makerspaces

- **Batch Print Missing Units:** In your slicer, duplicate the single tile to print 10 or 20 units in one job before class.
- **Support-Free Geometry:** All parts feature self-supporting angles ($\le 45^\circ$). Do not enable supports—they waste filament and create unnecessary cleanup.
- **Two-Color Debossed Letters:** Add a pause (M600) in PrusaSlicer / Bambu Studio at the layer where the debossed numbers start to swap filament colors for clean, high-contrast numerals.
- **For More Detailed Instructions:** See our comprehensive [Makerspace 3D Printing Guide](printables.md).

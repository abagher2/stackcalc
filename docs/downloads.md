## Recommended Print Settings

Every part in the StackCalc ecosystem is engineered for consumer 3D printers, calibrated on Prusa MK3S+/MK4/MINI+ and verified with PrusaSlicer (also compatible with Voron, Ender-3):

| Parameter | Specification | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nozzle Diameter** | `0.40 mm` (Standard) | Optimal balance between fine key legends and print speed |
| **Layer Height** | `0.20 mm` Standard (`0.12 mm` for keycaps/faceplates) | Tight interlocking tolerances for sliding dovetails and Gridfinity |
| **Supports** | **None (100% Support-Free)** | All overhangs $\le 45^\circ$; zero support removal scarring |
| **Infill** | `15% – 20%` (Gyroid or Grid) | Dimensional rigidity and acoustic resonance during desk drops |
| **Material** | PLA 2.0+ or PETG (`Chassis`), TPU 95A (`Keys & Pouches`) | Mil-Spec Shore 70D impact resistance and 400g snap actuation |

        <h3 class="ec-feature-title">The Mathematics of Pip Mass Balance</h3>
        <p class="ec-feature-desc leading-relaxed">
          Standard cheap dice drill deep holes for the 6-face and only 1 shallow hole for the 1-face, shifting the center of gravity and heavily biasing outcomes. StackCalc dice scale pip radii inversely (\(r_n = 1.8 / \sqrt{n}\)), ensuring the total displaced plastic volume is identical across all 6 faces.
        </p>

  <section class="ec-section">
    <h2 class="ec-section-title">Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions</h2>
    <p class="ec-section-subtitle">Assembling the modular probability workstation.</p>

    <div class="ec-highlights-list">
      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">1</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Seat the Base Box:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Place the 1x1 Gridfinity storage box (42.0 &times; 42.0 &times; 36.0 mm) on the desk or into a master modular math tray.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">2</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Select and Insert the Sector Dial:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Choose from the 4 included modular dials (Uniform 10-Sector 0&ndash;9, 4-Color Quadrant, Biased Distribution, or Arithmetic Operations). Drop the dial disc into the circular rebate on the spinner lid.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">3</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Mount the Needle Pointer:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Lower the balanced needle pointer over the central spindle pin. The micro-clearance bore (0.15 mm clearance) allows 30+ seconds of free rotary spin with zero binding.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">4</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Snap the Retention Cap:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Press the retention cap onto the spindle tip until it clicks. The cap secures the needle against classroom drops while preserving free rotation.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

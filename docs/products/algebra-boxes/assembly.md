  <section class="ec-section">
    <h2 class="ec-section-title">Step-by-Step Factoring Stage Setup</h2>
    <p class="ec-section-subtitle">Setting up the algebraic workstation.</p>

    <div class="ec-highlights-list">
      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">1</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Retrieve Rails from 3x1 Box:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Open the modular 3x1 Gridfinity storage box (126.0 &times; 42.0 &times; 36.0 mm). Remove the Base Rail (horizontal axis) and Height Rail (vertical axis) from the upper storage rebate.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">2</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Interlock the 90&deg; Corner:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Align the tenon tab on the Height Rail with the mortise slot on the Base Rail. Slide them together horizontally until they form a rigid 90-degree corner L-frame on the tabletop.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">3</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Tile Footprint System:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">The tiles adhere strictly to the Gridfinity miniblock ecosystem:
            <ul class="list-disc pl-5 mt-1 text-xs text-gray-300 space-y-1">
              <li><strong>Unit Tile (1):</strong> \(19.0 \times 19.0 \times 7.0\text{ mm}\) (1 unit square).</li>
              <li><strong>Rod Tile (x):</strong> \(38.6 \times 19.0 \times 7.0\text{ mm}\) (spans 2 unit lengths).</li>
              <li><strong>Square Tile (\(x^2\)):</strong> \(38.6 \times 38.6 \times 7.0\text{ mm}\) (spans a \(2 \times 2\) area).</li>
            </ul>
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

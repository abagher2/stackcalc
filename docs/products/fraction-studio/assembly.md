        <h3 class="ec-feature-title">The Subtractive Joint Theorem</h3>
        <p class="ec-feature-desc leading-relaxed">
          Standard toy stacking blocks lose height at every joint because pins sink into sockets without compensation. Fraction Studio eliminates this:
        </p>
        <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-md font-mono text-xs text-sky-300 my-3">
          Total physical block height: H_block = d_socket + (84.0 mm / n)<br>
          Nesting depth: d_socket = 1.85 mm<br>
          Joint cancelation: d_socket - d_socket = 0.00 mm
        </div>
        <p class="ec-feature-desc text-xs text-gray-400">
          Because the 1.85 mm male base drops completely into the 1.85 mm female socket, every block contributes <em>exactly</em> \(84.0 / n\text{ mm}\) of visible vertical height.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

  <!-- Step-by-Step Instructions -->
  <section class="ec-section">
    <h2 class="ec-section-title">Step-by-Step Tabletop Setup</h2>
    <p class="ec-section-subtitle">Follow these instructions for stable classroom operation.</p>

    <div class="ec-highlights-list">
      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">1</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Desk Placement:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Place the base block directly on any flat school desk or tabletop. The chamfered 19.0&times;19.0 mm bottom footprint provides steady contact with zero wobble. Zero loose wooden trays or specialized boards are needed.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">2</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Orientation Alignment:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Align the front debossed fraction label (e.g. \(1/2\)) facing the student. The 4 faces provide continuous multi-notational mapping: Front = Fraction (\(1/2\)), Right = Decimal (\(0.5\)), Back = Percentage (\(50\%\)), Left = StackCalc RPN notation.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">3</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Interlocking Joint Insertion:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Lower the upper block vertically into the female socket of the lower block. The 45-degree lead-in chamfers naturally guide the block into position with a satisfying slip-fit (0.20 mm bilateral clearance).</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">4</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Flush Datum Verification:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">When building equivalent towers (such as two halves or four quarters), run a fingertip across the tops. The combined towers will be coplanar with the Whole tower within &plusmn;0.05 mm.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">5</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Back-of-Box Benchmark Gauge Comparator:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">For objective classroom verification without rulers, place the storage box behind your towers. The laser-sharp debossed scale provides direct waterline readouts for \(0, 1/6, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 5/6, 1\).</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

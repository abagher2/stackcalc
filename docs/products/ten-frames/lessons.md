  <!-- ==========================================================================
       LESSON 1: SUBITIZING & FIVE/TEN STRUCTURE
       ========================================================================== -->
  <section class="ec-section">
    <div class="p-6 bg-slate-900/60 border border-slate-700/60 rounded-xl">
      <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
        <span class="ec-pill ec-pill-category">Lesson 01</span>
        <span class="text-xs text-gray-400">Target Grades: K&ndash;1 &bull; Standard: CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.K.OA.A.3</span>
      </div>
      <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-2">Instant Subitizing: Recognizing 7 as 5 + 2</h2>
      <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 mb-6">
        <strong>Lesson Objective:</strong> Students identify quantities from 1 to 10 immediately without counting individual counters one-by-one, using the benchmark of 5.
      </p>

      <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-sky-400 mb-3">Step-by-Step Classroom Investigation</h3>
      <div class="space-y-4 mb-6">
        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">1.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Fill the Top Row (Five Benchmark):</strong> Place 5 counters in row 1 (sockets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). Point out: <em>"Whenever the top row is full, you do not need to count. You immediately know that is 5."</em>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">2.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Add Two in the Bottom Row:</strong> Place 2 counters in row 0 (sockets 6 and 7).
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">3.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Read the Number Structure:</strong> Ask: <em>"How many counters are there?"</em> Prompt the student to answer without pointing at each token: <em>"A full row of 5, plus 2 more, is 7."</em>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">4.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Count the Empty Spaces (Ten Complement):</strong> Ask: <em>"How many empty sockets remain?"</em> The student observes 3 empty spaces. Conclude: <em>"7 and 3 make 10!"</em>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Misconception Diagnostic Box -->
      <div class="p-4 bg-amber-950/40 border border-amber-600/40 rounded-lg mb-6">
        <h4 class="text-sm font-bold text-amber-300 mb-1">&Delta; Key Misconception Diagnostic: "The Persistent Finger Counter"</h4>
        <p class="text-xs text-amber-100/90 leading-relaxed mb-2">
          <strong>The Common Error:</strong> When shown a frame with 6 counters, the child still touches each token: "1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6."
        </p>
        <p class="text-xs text-amber-100/90 leading-relaxed m-0">
          <strong>The Physical Fix:</strong> Cover the top row with a flashcard. Ask: <em>"You know the top row has 5. What is 1 more than 5?"</em> Repeat with 2 more (7) and 3 more (8) until the child anchors their mental count directly from 5.
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Independent Transfer Task -->
      <div class="p-4 bg-slate-800/80 border border-slate-600/60 rounded-lg">
        <h4 class="text-sm font-bold text-emerald-400 mb-1">&check; Independent Transfer Check</h4>
        <p class="text-xs text-gray-200 leading-relaxed m-0">
          <strong>Prompt:</strong> Show the child a ten-frame with 8 counters for exactly 2 seconds, then cover it. Ask: <em>"How many counters did you see?"</em> Expected explanation: <em>"I saw 5 on top and 3 on the bottom, which is 8."</em>
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <!-- ==========================================================================
       LESSON 2: MAKING TEN WITH REGROUPING
       ========================================================================== -->
  <section class="ec-section">
    <div class="p-6 bg-slate-900/60 border border-slate-700/60 rounded-xl">
      <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
        <span class="ec-pill ec-pill-category">Lesson 02</span>
        <span class="text-xs text-gray-400">Target Grades: 1&ndash;2 &bull; Standard: CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.1.OA.B.3</span>
      </div>
      <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-2">The Make-Ten Strategy &amp; Regrouping: 8 + 5 = 13</h2>
      <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 mb-6">
        <strong>Lesson Objective:</strong> Solve addition problems that bridge 10 by physically moving counters to complete a ten, then transferring the bundle to the carry shelf.
      </p>

      <!-- Concept Illustration Card -->
      <div class="mb-6 rounded-lg overflow-hidden border border-slate-700/80 bg-slate-950/60 p-4">
        <img src="../../assets/renders/ten-frames-make-ten-concept.png" alt="Ten Frames Make-Ten Addition Strategy 8 + 5 = 10 + 3 = 13" class="rounded w-full mb-3" />
        <div class="text-xs text-gray-300 flex items-center justify-between">
          <span><strong>Physical Make-Ten Regrouping:</strong> 8 archival white tokens in Frame 1 receive 2 terracotta counters to complete the ten (making 10!), while 3 remaining terracotta counters occupy Frame 2. Total: \(10 + 3 = 13\).</span>
          <span class="text-emerald-400 font-mono">8 + 5 = 10 + 3 = 13</span>
        </div>
      </div>

      <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-sky-400 mb-3">Step-by-Step Classroom Investigation</h3>
      <div class="space-y-4 mb-6">
        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">1.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Represent the First Addend:</strong> Place 8 counters in the base ten-frame.
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">2.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Identify How Many to Make 10:</strong> Ask: <em>"How many counters do we need to make a full ten?"</em> The student sees 2 empty sockets.
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">3.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Decompose the Second Addend:</strong> The second number is 5. Have the student take 5 counters in hand. Take 2 of them to fill the empty sockets in the frame. Ask: <em>"How many are left in your hand?"</em> The student has 3 remaining.
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">4.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Execute the Physical Carry:</strong> The base ten-frame is now 100% full (ten ones). Consolidate the 10 and move 1 counter up to the +10 Elevated Carry Shelf. Place the 3 remaining counters in the active frame.
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex gap-3 text-sm text-gray-200">
          <span class="font-bold text-sky-400 min-w-6">5.</span>
          <div>
            <strong>Read the Regrouped Sum:</strong> Read the sum from the physical tiers: <em>"One ten and three ones is thirteen (\(10 + 3 = 13\))."</em>
            \[
            8 + 5 = 8 + (2 + 3) = (8 + 2) + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13
            \]
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Misconception Diagnostic Box -->
      <div class="p-4 bg-amber-950/40 border border-amber-600/40 rounded-lg mb-6">
        <h4 class="text-sm font-bold text-amber-300 mb-1">&Delta; Key Misconception Diagnostic: "Lost Carried Tens"</h4>
        <p class="text-xs text-amber-100/90 leading-relaxed mb-2">
          <strong>The Common Error:</strong> During written column addition, students write down 13 under the units column or forget to add the carried ten.
        </p>
        <p class="text-xs text-amber-100/90 leading-relaxed m-0">
          <strong>The Physical Fix:</strong> The elevated shelf gives "carrying" a literal vertical elevation. The student physically moves their arm up to place the token on the shelf, creating an embodied spatial memory of place-value regrouping.
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Independent Transfer Task -->
      <div class="p-4 bg-slate-800/80 border border-slate-600/60 rounded-lg">
        <h4 class="text-sm font-bold text-emerald-400 mb-1">&check; Independent Transfer Check</h4>
        <p class="text-xs text-gray-200 leading-relaxed m-0">
          <strong>Prompt:</strong> Solve \(7 + 6\) using the make-ten strategy. First state how many counters are needed to make 10, then how many remain, and show the final result on the dual-tier tray.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

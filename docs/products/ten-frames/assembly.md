        <h3 class="ec-feature-title">Why the Carry Shelf is Raised</h3>
        <p class="ec-feature-desc leading-relaxed">
          Traditional cardboard ten-frames force students to mentally imagine "carrying the one." In the StackCalc system, the carry position is physically elevated by 4.5 mm on an upper shelf. The act of "carrying a ten" is a literal, physical motion that builds motor memory.
        </p>

  <section class="ec-section">
    <h2 class="ec-section-title">Step-by-Step Setup Instructions</h2>
    <p class="ec-section-subtitle">Setting up the early numeracy workstation.</p>

    <div class="ec-highlights-list">
      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">1</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Desk Positioning:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Place the 3x1 Gridfinity tray (123.0 &times; 39.8 mm) flat on the school desk. The solid base prevents slide and tipping during rapid student placement.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">2</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Slide-In Counting Stage:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">The lower stage presents 10 square Gridfinity miniblock sockets organized into two rows of five: Row 1 (numbers 1 to 5) and Row 0 (numbers 6 to 10). Column 5 contains the dedicated CARRY socket.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">3</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Elevated Carry Shelf Stacking:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">For two-tier addition (tens and ones), stack the secondary shelf (numbers 11 to 20) on top of the base tray using the standard Gridfinity stacking lip (1.8 mm rebate).</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ec-highlight-item">
        <span class="ec-highlight-icon">4</span>
        <div>
          <strong class="text-white">Reversible Counters:</strong>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300 m-0">Each 19.0 mm counter features a bottom base plug on Side A (seating into tray sockets) and a top stacking socket with debossed concentric tactile micro-grooves on Side B, allowing vertical stacking for multi-addend problems.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

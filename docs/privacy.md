# Privacy Policy

<div class="inline-block px-3 py-1 mb-6 border border-accentCyan text-accentCyan font-mono text-xs tracking-wider">// LEGAL PROTOCOL · ZERO DATA COLLECTION · EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 14, 2026</div>

At **StackCalc**, we believe mathematics is personal, foundational, and should never be accompanied by surveillance, telemetry, or advertising. Whether you are using **StackCalc32** on iOS, iPadOS, or Apple Watch, or following the physical calculator project, **we collect zero personal data**.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection

- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** We do not track keystrokes, calculation frequency, app session length, feature adoption, or user workflows.
- **No Device Identifiers:** We do not read or transmit IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), IDFV (Identifier for Vendors), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
- **No Diagnostics or Crash Reports:** We do not bundle third-party crash tracking or diagnostic SDKs.
- **No Biometrics or Health Data:** While running standalone on Apple Watch, StackCalc does not access Apple Health, heart rate sensors, workout data, or any biometric sensors.

---

## 2. 100% Local On-Device Computation

Every calculation, stack operation, variable memory register, equation, and statistical curve is evaluated exclusively within the local memory of your device:

- **RPN Stack & Memory Registers:** Stack levels (X, Y, Z, T, and extended levels), storage registers (0–9, A–Z), and financial registers are stored in local device memory only.
- **Continuous Memory:** In accordance with physical calculator behavior, values stored in continuous memory remain preserved locally between app sessions until manually cleared (`CLEAR` &rarr; `Continuous Memory`) or until the application is removed from your device.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** The application operates completely offline. No network connectivity is required, requested, or used.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The application contains **zero third-party software development kits (SDKs)**, tracking pixels, ad networks, or third-party APIs. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose any user data to third parties under any circumstance.

---

## 4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & Global Standards)

StackCalc is designed for learners of all ages, from elementary school students exploring exact fraction manipulatives to university students and professionals performing advanced scientific analysis.

- Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we strictly comply with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, and California privacy laws.
- We do not require account creation, registration, or login.
- Children can safely use StackCalc in classrooms, exams, and at home without privacy risk.

---

## 5. Website Privacy (stackcalc.io)

Our documentation and storefront website (`https://www.stackcalc.io`) is hosted via GitHub Pages. Standard server access logs (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent headers) may be automatically processed by GitHub to ensure infrastructure security and deliver the web service. We do not inject tracking cookies or third-party marketing trackers on this documentation site.

StackCalc does not currently process direct physical-product purchases through this website. If a future product links to a named marketplace, that marketplace's privacy policy will describe its checkout, payment, and shipping-data practices.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because StackCalc collects no data on remote servers:

- There is no remote account or database record to delete.
- To erase all local state, press **Yellow Shift** + `C` (`CLEAR`) &rarr; tap `CLALL` inside the app, or uninstall the application from your Apple device.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect future updates or legal compliance. Any changes will be posted immediately to this URL (`https://www.stackcalc.io/privacy/`) with an updated effective date.

Duplex Folding & Unfolding Choreography (Pages 01 to 08)

1. Print Duplex: Print Side A (Pages 01–04) and Side B (Pages 05–08) front-and-back on standard Letter (11 x 8.5 in) paper.
2. Bench Fold 1 (Horizontal): Hold Side A facing you and fold the bottom half (03–04) behind the top half (01–02).
3. Bench Fold 2 (Vertical): Fold the right half (02) behind the left half (01). Page 01 is now on the front cover!
4. Customer Unfolding Experience: Open right to read 01 → 02, open down to read 03 → 04 (two complete hands-on examples!), then flip over for 05 (Challenges) → 06 (Pack-Up Map) → 07 (Ecosystem) → 08 (Part IDs & Video QR).

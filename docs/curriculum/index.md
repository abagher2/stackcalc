Welcome to the **StackCalc Learning Lab Curriculum Hub**. At StackCalc, we believe that mathematical comprehension should not begin behind a flat pane of glass or inside abstract parentheses soup. 

Our instructional system bridges Jerome Bruner’s **Concrete-Representational-Abstract (CRA)** framework with the mechanical clarity of **Reverse Polish Notation (RPN)**:
1. **Concrete:** Students assemble, measure, and verify quantities on physical desk workbench lids.
2. **Representational:** Students diagram tape models, area grids, and number bonds in structured lab sheets.
3. **Abstract:** Students formalize the calculation on 4-line live operational stacks (`SC-6 Elementary` and `SC-32 Scientific`), where the physical drop and lift of numbers mirror physical reality.

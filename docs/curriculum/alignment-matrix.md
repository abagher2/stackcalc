This alignment matrix provides classroom educators and curriculum coordinators with an exact, cross-referenced mapping of **StackCalc Published Block Studios (`PLACE-01`, `FRAC-01`, `PRIME-01`)**, **Handheld RPN Calculator Keystroke Flows**, and the corresponding chapters in major national curricula.

> **Concept $\rightarrow$ Blocks (When Applicable) $\rightarrow$ Calculator:** Every lesson introduces the mathematical concept first through its stack structure (Waiting Shelf `Y` and Workbench `X`), models the concept physically with one of our three published block kits (**[Place Value Studio (`PLACE-01`)](../products/place-value/index.md)**, **[Fraction Studio (`FRAC-01`)](../products/fraction-studio/index.md)**, or **[Prime Factor Studio (`PRIME-01`)](../products/prime-factor-studio/index.md)**) when applicable, and verifies the calculation on the **SC-6 Elementary** or **SC-32 Scientific** 4-level stack.

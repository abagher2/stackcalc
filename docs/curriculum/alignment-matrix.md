This alignment matrix provides classroom educators and curriculum coordinators with an exact, cross-referenced mapping of **StackCalc Physical Manipulatives**, **Handheld RPN Calculator Keystroke Flows**, and the corresponding chapters in major national curricula.

> **Our Hardware-First Pedagogy:** StackCalc does not manufacture generic plastic toys like geoboards, plastic tree connectors, or plastic stack holders. Every manipulative in our learning lab is an authentic, precision Gridfinity instrument designed to pair directly with a dedicated button or calculation mode on our **SC-6 Elementary** and **SC-32 Scientific** RPN calculators. The calculator's default 4-line reflective LCD (with toggleable 2-stack mode for number racks and dice) **is** the live operational stack.

    StackCalc Curriculum & Standards Guide

    PEDAGOGICAL ARCHITECTURE // CRA FRAMEWORK ● CCSS-M • BRIDGES • BIG IDEAS • SINGAPORE DIMENSIONS
    📖 Master Guide ⚡ RPN Power Guide 📝 120 Station Worksheets 🎯 Standards Matrix 🧑‍🏫 Teacher Guide 🏆 UIL Competition

    Welcome to the StackCalc Learning Lab Curriculum Hub. At StackCalc, we believe that mathematical comprehension should not begin behind a flat pane of glass or inside abstract parentheses soup.

    Our instructional system bridges Jerome Bruner’s Concrete-Representational-Abstract (CRA) framework with the mechanical clarity of Reverse Polish Notation (RPN): 1. Concrete: Students assemble, measure, and verify quantities on physical desk workbench lids. 2. Representational: Students diagram tape models, area grids, and number bonds in structured lab sheets. 3. Abstract: Students formalize the calculation on 4-line live operational stacks (SC-6 Elementary and SC-32 Scientific), where the physical drop and lift of numbers mirror physical reality.

    The Four Curricular Alignments

    StackCalc literature is built to drop directly into existing school district pacing guides across four primary curricula:

    FRAMEWORK 01 • Standard Alignment

    Common Core State Standards (CCSS-M)

    Comprehensive domain coverage spanning Counting & Cardinality (CC), Operations & Algebraic Thinking (OA), Number & Operations in Base Ten (NBT), Fractions (NF), Expressions & Equations (EE), and High School Functions & Modeling.

    Explore Common Core Standards →
    FRAMEWORK 02 • Visual Models

    Bridges in Mathematics

    Native support for Bridges visual models: Number Racks (Rekenrek) map directly to our 10-Frame Carry Shelves; Open Arrays and Tile Models map to the Algebra Factoring Stage; Double Number Lines match our Fraction Waterline Comparator.

    Explore Bridges Cross-References →
    FRAMEWORK 03 • Inquiry & Exploration

    Big Ideas Math (Larson & Boswell)

    Aligned with Big Ideas "Essential Questions" and exploratory investigations. Students use physical manipulatives to discover geometric and algebraic properties before transitioning to procedural calculator fluency.

    Explore Big Ideas Units →
    FRAMEWORK 04 • CPA & Bar Modeling

    Singapore Dimensions Math

    Direct implementation of Singapore Math’s Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract progression. Fraction Studio blocks serve as physical bar models (tape diagrams); Math Dominoes provide tactile Number Bonds; and RPN stacks mirror mental math branching.

    Explore Singapore Dimensions →

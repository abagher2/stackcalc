    From the Teacher's Manual: Unit Lab Sheet

    Unit 03b • Addition Doubles

    Most traditional math teacher manuals treat dominoes as games of chance. In the StackCalc curriculum, the Domino Studio is an active laboratory tool connecting dot subitizing to mental addition doubles and discrete probability distributions.

    STACKCALC MATH LABS // SERIES 03b STANDARDS: CCSS 1.OA.C.6 • 2.OA.B.2 • TEKS 111.3.b.3 PERIOD: 40 MIN STATION

    Lab 03b: Tactile Doubles, Part-Whole Decomposition & Domino Probability

    Investigation Goal: Prove addition doubles visually and kinesthetically using the doubling snap lid and 12-frame tray. Model the mental math transition from doubles (\(6+6=12\)) to near-doubles (\(6+7=13\)), then verify sum distributions across all 28 tiles.

    □ PHASE 1: CONCRETE SPATIAL EXPLORATION (DOUBLING LID STAGE)

    Sort the 30 domino tiles on your desk. Separate the 7 double tiles (\([0|0]\) through \([6|6]\)). Snap each double into its labeled lid socket:

    Socket 1: 2+2 = 4 Snap the \([2|2]\) tile into the lid. Notice 2 dots on each side equal 4 total pips.
    Socket 2: 5+5 = 10 Snap the \([5|5]\) tile into the lid. Fills the first 10 positions of a double ten-frame!
    Socket 3: 6+6 = 12 Snap the \([6|6]\) tile into the lid. Fills the entire 12-frame regrouping tray.
    ≡ PHASE 2: REPRESENTATIONAL DATA & 12-FRAME DECOMPOSITION

    Seat dominoes into the 6x2 tray. Record how sums bridge through 10 using the permanent tactile demarcation line:

    Domino Tile [A|B] Addition Expression Base-10 Decomposition Total Pip Sum Category
    [4|4] \(4 + 4\) \(8\) (Within 10) 8 Pure Double
    [5|5] \(5 + 5\) 10 + 0 (Full Ten) 10 Ten Benchmark
    [6|5] \(6 + 5 = (5 + 5) + 1\) 10 + 1 (Crosses Divider) 11 Near Double (+1)
    [6|6] \(6 + 6 = 10 + 2\) 10 + 2 (Full Tray) 12 Max Double
    Δ PHASE 3: ABSTRACT VERIFICATION (CALCULATOR EXECUTION)

    1. Verify addition doubles rhythm on the SC-6 Elementary using skip-counting:

    2 REP REP REP REP REP → LCD Displays: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 (Doubles Rhythm)

    2. High School Combinatorics on SC-32: Calculate total tiles in a double-\(n\) set (\(n=6\)):

    7 ENTER 2 nCr 7 + → X-Register: 28.0000 (✓ 28 Unique Dominoes)
    STACK X: 28.0000 (TOTAL DOMINOES) SAMPLE SPACE: 28 TILES THEORETICAL SUMS: 0 TO 12 ✓ EXACT MATCH TO DOUBLE-SIX SET
    Teacher Diagnostic Note: When students struggle with near-doubles (e.g. \(6 + 7\)), they often revert to counting by ones on fingers. Have them locate the \([6|6]\) double tile first (\(6+6=12\)), snap it into the doubling lid, then add 1 more pip using a unit counter to get 13. This builds mental math chunking rather than slow procedural counting.

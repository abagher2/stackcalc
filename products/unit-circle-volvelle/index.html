    Rotate the top disc on its flush brass eyelet pivot. Align the cursor window with standard benchmark angles. Observe how the aperture isolates exact coordinate pairs and sign transitions:

    Quadrant I (\(60^\circ / \frac{\pi}{3}\)) Aperture reveals \(\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\). Both \(x\) and \(y\) are positive.
    Quadrant II (\(120^\circ / \frac{2\pi}{3}\)) Aperture reveals \(\left(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\). Cosine switches to negative.
    Quadrant III (\(210^\circ / \frac{7\pi}{6}\)) Aperture reveals \(\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}\right)\). Tangent is positive (\(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\)).

    STACKCALC MATH LABS // SERIES 04 STANDARDS: CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 • TEKS 111.39.c.8 PERIOD: 45 MIN STATION

    Lab 04: Geometric Area Model & Polynomial Factoring

    Investigation Goal: Construct physical quadratic polynomials inside the \(150 \times 150\,\text{mm}\) framing tray using unit, linear, and square tiles. Discover the binomial factors \((x + a)(x + b)\) spatially, then verify root solutions on the SC-32 RPN stack.

    □ PHASE 1: CONCRETE SPATIAL EXPLORATION (MANIPULATIVE STAGE)

    Build the trinomial expression \(x^2 + 5x + 6\) inside the staging tray:

    • Place one large \(x^2\) tile (\(60 \times 60\,\text{mm}\)) in the upper-left datum corner.
    • Arrange five \(x\)-bars (\(60 \times 12\,\text{mm}\)) and six unit tiles (\(12 \times 12\,\text{mm}\)) into a solid, gap-free rectangle.
    • Observe the outer boundary dimensions: the horizontal width is \((x + 3)\); the vertical height is \((x + 2)\).
    ≡ PHASE 2: REPRESENTATIONAL DATA & FACTOR GRID RECORD
    Target Trinomial Physical Tile Inventory Boundary Width Boundary Height Factored Form
    \(x^2 + 5x + 6\) \(1 \times x^2,\; 5 \times x,\; 6 \times 1\) \(x + 3\) \(x + 2\) \((x + 3)(x + 2)\)
    \(x^2 + 7x + 12\) \(1 \times x^2,\; 7 \times x,\; 12 \times 1\) \(x + 4\) \(x + 3\) \((x + 4)(x + 3)\)
    Δ PHASE 3: ABSTRACT VERIFICATION (SC-32 HARDWARE EVALUATION)

    Verify root \(x = -2\) by evaluating \(x^2 + 5x + 6\) on the SC-32 RPN stack:

    2 ± ENTER x² 2 ± 5 × + 6 + → X-Register: 0.0000 (✓ Root Confirmed)
    STACK X: 0.0000 (POLYNOMIAL VALUE) ROOT EVALUATED: x = -2.0000 PHYSICAL RECTANGLE: 72mm × 84mm (x=60mm datum) ✓ GEOMETRIC PROOF CONFIRMED
    Teacher Diagnostic Note: When students factor \(x^2 + 5x + 6\), they commonly confuse factors of 6 (\(6 \times 1\) vs \(3 \times 2\)). If they attempt \((x + 6)(x + 1)\), the tiles physically spill outside the boundary of the \(5x\) bar inventory, providing self-correcting visual feedback before any symbolic test mistakes occur.

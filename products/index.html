    The Architecture of Understanding: Postfix vs. In-Fix

    Why physical stacking and Reverse Polish Notation outperform legacy calculating tools.

    LEGACY IN-FIX COMPUTING
    • Parentheses Traps: Formulas are entered as abstract strings with invisible precedence rules and constant syntax errors.
    • Ephemeral Black Boxes: Numbers vanish behind plastic membranes; intermediate states cannot be inspected or verified.
    • Disposable Hardware: Thin injection polystyrene, non-repairable assemblies, and battery leaks destined for landfills.
    • Recurring Software Rent: Cloud apps and paid emulator licenses ($15/seat/year) laden with notifications and ads.
    THE STACKCALC HARDWARE PHILOSOPHY
    • Pure Physical Postfix: Zero parentheses. Numbers are pushed onto visible shelves; operations consume them in order of physical causality.
    • 1:1 Spatial Mapping: Physical manipulative blocks on your desk map directly to the 4-line hardware LCD stack.
    • Heirloom Craftsmanship: Precision matte polymer with debossed geometry, snap-dome microswitches, and Shore 90A TPU keys.
    • Zero Subscriptions, 100% Repairable: Buy once, own forever. 100% open-source 3MF files to print replacement parts anytime.

// SPECIFICATION & PROCUREMENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

[01] Are StackCalc hardware calculators permitted on standardized tests (SAT, ACT, AP)? +

Yes. The SC-32 and SC-6 are engineered strictly as non-programmable, non-graphing (in hardware firmware) scientific calculators without Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cameras, or QWERTY keyboards, making them 100% compliant with SAT, ACT, and AP testing regulations.

[02] How does the "Anti-Heartbreak" Lifetime Replacement Guarantee work? +

Classrooms and busy homes lose parts under desks and couches. Rather than forcing anyone to buy an entire new kit when a single counter, tile, or fraction block vanishes, our Anti-Heartbreak Guarantee ships individual matching replacement pieces for just $2.49 with free envelope shipping. Every kit includes an archival reference card with exact part IDs for 1-click replacement.

[03] Can we 3D print our own kits using school maker spaces? +

Yes! All CAD files (.stl, .3mf, .step) are 100% free open-source downloads on this site. If your school has 3D printers, you can print the entire manipulative library completely free.

[04] What makes Reverse Polish Notation (RPN) superior for students? +

RPN evaluates equations sequentially without parentheses. When a student calculates (5 + 3) × 2, they press 5, ENTER, 3, +, and watch the intermediate sum 8 appear instantly before multiplying by 2. It reinforces order of operations through visible stack registers.

[05] How do we pay with a School Purchase Order or Tax-Exempt W-9? +

Simply email your purchase order or request an official quote using the interactive requisition builder above or at quotes@stackcalc.io. We accept Net-30 payment terms, provide our Federal W-9 form and vendor onboarding packet within 24 hours, and ship via standard parcel carrier (USPS Priority / UPS Ground) directly to your school front office (Attn: Math Dept / Room #). No freight loading dock is ever required.

[06] Can we order physical spiral-bound curriculum workbooks without printing them ourselves? +

Yes. In addition to 100% free downloadable PDFs with our Perpetual Classroom Reproduction License, we offer premium lay-flat coil-bound workbooks printed on 80# opaque paper. They lay completely flat on student desks for effortless pencil-and-paper work and can be ordered directly on-demand via our storefront or included with district fleet orders.

[07] Why do StackCalc instruments cost more than commodity plastic sets on Amazon or wooden blocks? +

Commodity school sets are hollow, injection-molded plastics with loose ±1.5mm tolerances and printed ink that rubs off within weeks. Traditional wooden sets warp and swell with room humidity. StackCalc instruments are micrometer-calibrated to ±0.05mm so fractional equivalence is physically flush to the touch. They are molded from dense, non-toxic organic biopolymers with deep permanent debossing that never fades, housed in self-auditing 3-second clean-up boxes, and backed by lifetime $2.49 exact piece replacement. You buy an instrument once for your child's entire schooling, and it can be handed down to siblings.

[08] I'm a parent, not a math teacher. Can I use this with my child without hours of prep? +

Yes, absolutely. Every kit includes zero-jargon, laminated 5-Minute Parent-Child Playcards. The geometry is inherently self-correcting—for example, two 1/4 blocks physically match the exact height of a 1/2 block. Children test ideas with their own hands and discover mathematical principles through guided play in under 10 minutes, with zero frustration or adult preparation required.

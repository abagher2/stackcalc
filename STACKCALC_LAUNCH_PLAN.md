# StackCalc launch plan

## Purpose

Prepare one truthful, reusable public record for StackCalc while the RP2350 board is in fabrication. StackCalc is the canonical home for public STL packs, teacher PDFs, app links, and detailed engineering articles. Hackaday is the build log and discussion surface. Etsy is the first planned sales channel for physical accessories and teaching kits. No implementation source is part of the public release.

## Current baseline

- The iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch applications are available.
- The browser path exercises a compiled firmware build in an RP2350 emulator; this is not board bring-up evidence.
- The KiCad RP2350 board design is in fabrication. No assembled, powered StackCalc board is on the desk.
- The current calculator photos show a printed, tool-free mechanical prototype with unlabelled keys.
- The Learning Lab has public teacher PDFs and STL packs. Classroom and repeat-print results are still required before product claims.

## Content rule

Each topic starts as one StackCalc Markdown article. Hackaday uses the same factual body, evidence, captions, and links, reformatted for its project-log layout. A feed title, excerpt, image order, and discussion question may change; factual claims and their prototype stage may not. A post that describes emulation, CAD, or a simulation must say so plainly and must not imply a physical measurement.

## Release sequence

### Phase 1 — Establish the public record

Complete the BLOG and HDA issues that define the canonical post map, reconcile public claims to current evidence, and make every public image/caption identify whether it shows an app, simulation, or printed specimen.

**Exit gate:** A reader can tell within the first screen of every entry what is real, simulated, fabricated, and unmeasured.

### Phase 2 — Finish the Hackaday launch package

Use the finished public source material to complete the project Details, components, files, proof reel, build instructions, and a measured publication cadence. Keep the project unpublished until the project page and the initial log sequence meet the same status standard.

**Exit gate:** The project has a truthful hero image, a visible proof reel, useful files, current instructions, a clear next milestone, and no stale pre-fabrication language.

### Phase 3 — Capture board-arrival evidence

When the board arrives, record the first physical fit and bring-up session before changing any claim from design/emulation to hardware behavior. Publish the specimen revision, board revision, test sequence, observations, failures, photographs, and repeat work.

**Exit gate:** The project separates board-fit evidence, powered firmware evidence, power evidence, and keypad evidence. A successful boot is not treated as completion of all four.

### Phase 4 — Launch Etsy-ready accessory products

Start with products that already have a coherent physical purpose: a printed Learning Lab kit, a puzzle stand, and a printed teacher workbook or differentiated classroom pack. Keep the downloadable teacher PDFs free on StackCalc. Do not list the physical calculator until its board, keypad, enclosure, packaging, and support promise have been physically validated.

**Exit gate:** Each product has a named revision, real product photos, measured contents, material and safety information, fulfillment lead time, replacement/return procedure, and a test order through the selected marketplace.

### Phase 5 — Replace Shopify deliberately

Do not direct buyers to a new marketplace until its seller obligations have been confirmed for the operating location and every direct Shopify purchase path has been removed or redirected. Update the public legal/support pages only after the marketplace decision and support policy are concrete.

**Exit gate:** StackCalc has one live purchase path per SKU, no stale Shopify language, and a tested buyer-support route.

## Product boundaries

| Product | Intended first channel | Evidence required before listing | Not a current claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Learning Lab physical kit | Etsy | repeatable prints, contents/fit review, real photos, classroom-material review | classroom outcomes or universal printer fit |
| Puzzle stand | Etsy | final geometry, repeatable print/assembly, real photos, packaging fit | shipping protection or load rating without tests |
| Printed workbook / expanded teacher pack | Etsy | differentiated contents and license, print proof, real photos | sale of the already-free PDF downloads |
| StackCalc32 calculator | hold | board fit, display/keypad integration, powered bring-up, labeling, packaging and support validation | current availability, battery performance, or finished hardware |

## Publishing rhythm

Publish no more than one substantial Hackaday log per real decision or measured milestone. Initial sequence: project overview, Watch/RPN design constraint, shared core and emulator, tool-free mechanical assembly, membrane study, Learning Lab, stand/packaging, then board arrival. Use comments to request a narrow engineering critique, such as a rail geometry, membrane test fixture, or board-fit checklist.

## Success measures

- Every public claim has an artifact, observation, or explicitly named next test.
- StackCalc and Hackaday links resolve to the same current stage and assets.
- A prospective Etsy buyer can identify exactly what is physical, what is included, when it ships, and how support works.
- The calculator is never offered for sale before physical validation supports the listing.
